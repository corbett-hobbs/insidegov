# Immigration policy markers: draft for review

Status: **draft, not built.** This is the third kind of context in draft rule 7d (`ARCHITECTURE_MAP.md`): contested, multi-factor causes are never stated as causes on the page. The dated policy actions go on the chart as numbered markers instead, so a reader can see what was in effect when a line turned, with no claim about what each action did to the numbers.

## The selection test (applied to every administration alike)

A marker is added only for:

- a presidential document (an executive order, proclamation or Presidential Determination), a cabinet secretary's memo, or a final or interim final rule,
- that changes who is processed, how, or who is prioritized,
- at the border (encounters card) or in interior enforcement (removals card).

The test runs over the chart's whole window (FY1980 on), not just recent years. Each marker card gives the instrument, dates (signed, effective, ended), a one-line plain description of what it directed, its status (in effect, superseded, enjoined, revoked), the primary document, and a nonpartisan summary (CRS, GAO, CBO or the Migration Policy Institute) where one exists. Each card ends with the rule 10m line: "Shown for when it was in effect; this is not a claim about its effect on the numbers." Markers that sit close together merge into one numbered pill, as the refugee chart's do.

Markers already on these charts (counting changes) stay as they are: Title 42 begins and ends, CBP One appointments, port figures begin, ship crews.

## Not markers, by the test

- Conditions abroad (Venezuela, Central America, Haiti), the U.S. job market, the pandemic as an economic shock: these are not dated government actions, and their weight is disputed. If they are mentioned at all, it would be in Data notes as "researchers point to several factors", naming nonpartisan sources and no single cause. My recommendation is to leave them out.
- Parole programs (CHNV, Jan 2023) already appear through the CBP One / parole counting marker.

## Open questions for review

2. Each "to verify" row needs its primary document and a CRS or MPI summary before building.
