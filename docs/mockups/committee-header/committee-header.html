HOUSE COMMITTEE

House Committee on Ways and Means

Republican control · 26R·19D

Chair: Jason Smith (R)

Ranking Member: Richard Neal (D)

45 members in the 119th Congress

About

The Committee on Ways and Means is the chief tax-writing committee of the United States House of Representatives. It has jurisdiction over all taxation, tariffs, and other revenue-raising measures, as well as programs including Social Security, unemployment benefits, and Medicare. It has long been regarded as the most prestigious committee of the House.
