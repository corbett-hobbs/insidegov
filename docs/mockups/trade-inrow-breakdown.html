Chapters and uses, side by side

The four-digit product list, the By product / By use toggle and the “From product to use” card are all gone. One card, two ranked lists on one dollar scale: chapters (what the goods are, by tariff chapter) and uses (what they are for, Census end-use categories). Tap a line: the other list dims to the lines it is linked to, and the line opens to a coloured bar and short list (the chapter’s uses, or the use’s chapters) above its top five countries. Real 2025 numbers, all countries.

What changes
  1. Removed: the top-products list (four-digit HS headings), the By product / By use toggle, and the “From product to use” Sankey card. The product layer behind them goes too: the 1,200-name heading table, the per-product partner files and most of the special-entries footnote.
  2. One view, two columns: Chapters | Uses, on one dollar scale. On phones they stack, each a fixed-height scroll box, as now.
  3. Tap a chapter: the uses it feeds stay lit and the rest dim; it opens to “What it’s used for” (top 5 uses + Other) and the top five countries.
  4. Tap a use: the chapters behind it stay lit; it opens to “Where it comes from” (top 5 chapters + Other) and the top five countries.
  5. Each breakdown row has one small context line: “93% of all computer accessories imports” (a use, for a chapter) or “42% of this chapter’s imports” (a chapter, for a use).
  6. With a country picked, both lists still rank that country’s trade; rows don’t open (as now).
Trade-off: the catch-all uses (“Other vehicle parts”, “Finished metal shapes”, “Cell phones & household goods”, about a quarter of dollars) lose the product detail that explained them. Their chapter breakdown is the only hint left.
Open: whether imports should print a duty rate on use rows too (computable nationally from the same file; chapters already have one).
