Checkbox filters instead of single-select dropdowns

The idea. Where a filter's options are parts of a whole and one of them is big enough to swamp the rest, a dropdown can only answer "show me just this one". A checkbox list also answers "show me everything except this one", and "these three". Same bar footprint (one control, panel overlays the page), same state the charts already read.

  • Laws · Policy area: Commemorations is 3,137 of 12,620 laws (25%), and 255 of the 713 laws in the 100th Congress (36%). Untick it and every chart, the heatmap and the list show what Congress did beyond naming post offices and commemorative days.
  • The panel starts open below with Commemorations unticked. Everything is live: tick, untick, All / Clear, and the one-tap "Skip commemorations".
  • The mock uses real counts from pipeline/output. Colours are the site's --laws-* palette (checkbox accent is --accent like the existing Major laws checkbox).
