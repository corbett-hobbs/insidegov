Making the Presidency's seven pages easier to find

Today the pages share one row with the branch tabs, in the same mono caps, and on a typical laptop only four of the seven fit before the "+4" button. Below are four ways to fix that. Frames are drawn at desktop width with the real fonts; the page under each header is a stub.

Recommendation: A, plus D. A gives the pages their own full-width row under the branch tabs, so all seven show and the parent/child relationship reads at a glance (it's also what phones already do). D adds a "Next in Presidency" card at the bottom of every page, which is the natural way to flip through. C can be layered onto A later if you want readers to see Congress's pages while they're on a Presidency page.

NOW Today: one row, pages share it with the branches

Branches and pages look the same (mono caps), sit side by side, and the last pages hide behind "+4".

A Two rows: branches on top, this branch's pages underneath Recommended

The active branch tab opens into a tinted band that holds all of its pages, like a folder tab. Pages switch to sentence-case sans so they read as a different, lower level from the mono branch tabs. The band starts under its parent tab, so Congress's four pages hang under "Congress", the Court's two under "Supreme Court". The freed space on the right could hold the site search on every page (dashed, optional).

Good

  • All seven pages visible at every desktop width (about 730px of labels; the "+N" button stays only as a safety net near 768px).
  • Hierarchy is carried by position, type and the folder-tab shape, not just a divider.
  • Desktop matches the two-row phone header.

Cost

  • Header grows by ~46px before the pinned filter bar.
  • Puts a branch tint in the header, which today is kept neutral on purpose (globals.css comment); this would be a deliberate exception, for the band only.

C Each branch tab opens a menu of its pages, with blurbs

Hover, focus or click a branch tab (here, Congress while reading a Presidency page) to see every page in it, with the one-line blurb already written in lib/verticals.ts. Drawn on top of A; on its own it doesn't fix the hidden Presidency pages.

D Make "flipping through" part of the page itself
