What to decide (mock chrome, not part of the page)
  1. Page length (plan open question 1). The page would hold 8 cards (3 ICE, 3 refugee, 2 here), and session H adds a visas card or two. Recommendation: keep one page for now with a third sub-header and jump links (mocked), then split in two when H lands: “Coming in” (green cards, refugees, visas) and “Removals” (ICE). The split changes the nav, so I would rather do it once with H than twice.
  2. Section order (open question 2). Recommendation: coming in first (green cards, then refugees), removals last: the h1 already reads “In and Out”, and green cards are the largest number on the page by far. This moves the ICE block, which has been first so far; use the toggle above to compare.
  3. Naturalizations: B, the pill toggle (agreed). One chart at a time, the same Green cards / Naturalizations control as the country card, and no extra height. A second panel (A) and a twin-axis line were rejected; the toggle above still lets you compare A.
  4. Window. The one pinned slider stays FY2003–FY2025. The OHSS series reaches FY1977 (totals) and FY1999 (classes), but widening the slider would leave the ICE and refugee cards empty for the early years. No FY2026 slot: OHSS has not reported it.
  5. Six class colours, not seven. OHSS prints refugees and asylees as separate classes; the mock draws them as one “Refugees and asylees” colour so the stack reuses the six validated sector slots (as foreign aid does). The pipeline keeps both; the tooltip could split them. Say if you want seven.
  6. FY2025 country basis. FY2025 is by country of nationality (the only source), earlier years by country of birth. The list says so and drops the change column for FY2025 rather than compare the two. The card does not hide FY2025.
