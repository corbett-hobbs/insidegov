Notes: To protect privacy, table cells are rounded to the nearest ten. The totals may not sum due to rounding. Data include all DHS removals, returns, and expulsions. FY 1976 includes the 15 months from July 1, 1975 to September 30, 1976 because the end date of fiscal years was changed from June 30 to September 30.

Source: Office of Homeland Security Statistics.

  1. Returns are the confirmed movement of an inadmissible or deportable alien out of the United States not based on an order of removal. Administrative returns include crew members detained on board their vessels since 2009 and also include administrative withdrawals in 2018 - 2023. Administrative withdrawals are withdrawals of application for admission that are terminated without prejudice. Enforcement returns are all returns with the exception of crew members detained on board their vessels and administrative withdrawals. All returns are reported as enforcement returns from 1927 to 2008. Data on returns are not available prior to 1927. ↩ Back
  2. Returns are the confirmed movement of an inadmissible or deportable alien out of the United States not based on an order of removal. Administrative returns include crew members detained on board their vessels since 2009 and also include administrative withdrawals in 2018 - 2023. Administrative withdrawals are withdrawals of application for admission that are terminated without prejudice. Enforcement returns are all returns with the exception of crew members detained on board their vessels and administrative withdrawals. All returns are reported as enforcement returns from 1927 to 2008. Data on returns are not available prior to 1927. ↩ Back
  3. Removals are the compulsory and confirmed movement of an inadmissible or deportable alien out of the United States based on an order of removal. An alien who is removed has administrative or criminal consequences placed on subsequent reentry owing to the fact of the removal. ↩ Back
  4. Expulsions on public health grounds under U.S. Code Title 42 in response to the COVID-19 pandemic. ↩ Back
  5. Total removals and returns include the sum of administrative returns, enforcement returns, and removals. ↩ Back
  6. Total enforcement repatriations include the sum of enforcement returns, removals, and Title 42 expulsions. ↩ Back
  7. Total Repatriations include the sum of administrative returns, enforcement returns, removals, and Title 42 expulsions. ↩ Back
