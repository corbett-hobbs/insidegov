                                          Nationwide Encounters

The goal of this data dictionary is to provide insight into how users should read and analyze the
raw data available for download.

I.        Tips when working with Encounter data:
       1. Statistical information is subject to change due to corrections, systems changes, change in
          data definition, additional information, or encounters pending final review.
       2. Nationwide numbers are calculated by adding together “Northern Land Border,”
          “Southwest Land Border,” and “Other” regions


II.       Data Dictionary

2
  FMUA represents the number of encountered individuals within the family unit, not the number of units. A Family
Unit is defined as a least one noncitizen minor child and at least one noncitizen adult parent or legal guardian. For
example, a family unit consisting of two or more individuals is encountered. (Continued on page two footer)
The data would show two or more individuals, not one family unit. Beginning in March 2020, FMUA data also
includes Title 42 Expulsions of a family group of at least one child and one related adult (regardless of whether the
adult and child met the definition of a family unit).
