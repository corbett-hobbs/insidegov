27	1 OFO administrative encounters are inadmissibility determinations that do not reflect attempted unlawful entry into the United States. Unlike enforcement encounters, they do not result in immigration proceedings. Examples of administrative encounters include crew members of foreign vessels required to remain aboard their ships, encounters resulting in parole dispositions, and FY 2018-2023 withdrawals of application for admission terminated without prejudice.
28	2 OFO enforcement encounters are inadmissibility determinations that reflect an attempted unlawful entry into the United States, including all determinations of inadmissibility not defined as administrative encounters. In FY 2020-2023, OFO encounters resulting in expulsions on public health grounds under U.S. Code Title 42 in response to the COVID-19 pandemic are also included.
